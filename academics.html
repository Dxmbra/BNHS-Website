Offered Tracks & Curricula

Junior High School (Grades 7–10)

Following the K-12 Basic Education Curriculum, focusing on core subjects like Mathematics, Science, English, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, and TLE.

Senior High School - Academic Track

Preparing learners for college with specialized strands including General Academic Strand (GAS) and Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS).

Senior High School - TVL Track

Equipping students with technical-vocational skills for immediate employment, entrepreneurship, or technical education progression.
