Academic Programs

Discover our comprehensive K-12 curriculum designed to prepare students for higher education, entrepreneurship, and professional success.

Offered Tracks & Curricula

Junior High School (Grades 7–10)

Building a strong foundational education under the K-12 Basic Education Curriculum, focusing on core competencies in Science, Mathematics, Languages, Social Studies, and TLE.

Senior High School – Academic Track

Preparing college-bound learners through specialized strands in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM), Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM), and Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS).

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) Track

Equipping students with practical, industry-ready technical and vocational skills tailored for immediate employment, entrepreneurship, or advanced technical training.
